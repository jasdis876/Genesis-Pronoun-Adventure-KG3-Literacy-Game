📖 Pronoun Review

I = me (👧🏾 Genesis says I)
he = boy / man (👨🏾 Daddy)
she = girl / woman (👧🏾 Genesis)
it = animal or thing (🐶 dog, ⚽ ball)
we = me and someone (👩🏾 Mommy and I)
they = more than one (👧🏾👦🏾 children)
